      How this works

      Gemma 4 runs on this computer. Your photos never leave it. The only network call this app makes is to the AI model on this same machine.

      The AI picks one of 9 fixed problem types for each photo and rates severity, urgency and confidence. It never writes dollar amounts or legal conclusions, and it gives no grade when it is unsure.

      Grading rule. Risk = severity × urgency. High confidence shows one letter. Medium confidence shows the letter and the next worse one. Low confidence shows no grade.

      Photo metadata (location, time, device) is removed when you upload. “Delete this session” erases your photos, findings and report.

      Cost ranges are placeholder estimates for the DC/MD/VA area.
